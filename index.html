Questions

Are my recordings stored on RecExport’s servers?

No. Files go straight from your iPhone to the storage you chose.

Will recording stop if I lock my iPhone?

No. Lock it and put it in your pocket.

Can I record without a signal?

Yes. The recording stays on your iPhone and uploads once you are back online.

Can I choose the folder?

Yes, one per destination, in Settings.
